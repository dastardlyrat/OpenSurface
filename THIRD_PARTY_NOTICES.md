# Third-Party Notices

## ReShade

Camera Obscura effects compile against ReShade and include `ReShade.fxh` by
name. OpenSurface does not bundle that file or the ReShade runtime.

The upstream `ReShade.fxh` currently identifies itself as `CC0-1.0`. The
ReShade runtime repository separately documents its own BSD 3-clause and
file-specific licensing. Those upstream terms continue to govern their files;
the OpenSurface MIT License does not replace them.

- ReShade shaders: https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders
- ReShade runtime: https://github.com/crosire/reshade
